## Tech Debt

### Phase 5 — Bindings: pyo3/wasm runtime coverage of `SendDestination::PuzzleHash`

**Severity:** low. Not a wiring break.

The `SendDestination::PuzzleHash` arm is exercised in **napi** at 10 call sites in `napi/__test__/action_system.spec.ts:174-240`, but **not** at runtime in pyo3 or wasm binding tests. The arm IS fully wired (descriptor exposes `SendDestination.puzzle_hash` factory; facade impl at `chia-sdk-bindings/src/action_system.rs:512`), and the descriptor↔facade drift audit reports 0 drift on 22 methods — so the pyo3/wasm `puzzle_hash` arm is **structurally correct**, just **untested at runtime** in those 2 binding test surfaces.

**Why it happened:** Phase 5 D-04 wrapped all pre-04.2 napi `Bytes32` callers (10 sites) in `SendDestination.puzzleHash(...)` to keep them compiling. pyo3 and wasm test surfaces had no pre-04.2 callers, so the migration exercise didn't replicate.

**Recommendation:** If Sage or another downstream consumer adopts pyo3 or wasm in production, add a `SendDestination::PuzzleHash` smoke test in those bindings (small one-off task; ~30 lines per binding). Not blocking v1.0 — the cross-language SP arm IS exercised in all 3 BIND-03 e2e tests, so the integration paths that matter most are covered.
